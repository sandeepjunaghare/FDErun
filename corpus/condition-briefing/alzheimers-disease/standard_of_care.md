Alzheimer's disease diagnosis combines cognitive assessment with biomarker confirmation of amyloid (by PET scan or
spinal fluid testing) when disease-modifying treatment is being considered.

Cholinesterase inhibitors are standard symptomatic treatment across Alzheimer's disease stages, and memantine is
added or used in moderate-to-severe disease.

Anti-amyloid monoclonal antibodies are an option for early Alzheimer's disease (mild cognitive impairment or mild
dementia) with confirmed amyloid. They require scheduled MRI monitoring for amyloid-related imaging abnormalities
(ARIA).

Alzheimer's disease care relies heavily on non-drug measures: caregiver education and support, safety planning,
management of behavioral symptoms, and advance care planning.

Alzheimer's disease care also manages contributing factors such as hearing loss, depression, vascular risk
factors, and medications that worsen cognition.
