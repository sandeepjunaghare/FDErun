RNK-41, from Rennick Nephro (fictional), is an oral aldosterone synthase inhibitor for chronic kidney disease in
Phase 3, tested on top of an SGLT2 inhibitor and an ACE inhibitor or ARB.

SRB-9, from Sorrell Bio (fictional), is a genetically targeted therapy for chronic kidney disease linked to
high-risk APOL1 variants, in Phase 2.

Rennick Nephro (fictional) is also testing a combination pill for chronic kidney disease that pairs RNK-41 with an
SGLT2 inhibitor to reduce pill burden.

The chronic kidney disease pipeline is moving toward stacking therapies with complementary mechanisms and toward
precision treatment for genetically defined subgroups.
