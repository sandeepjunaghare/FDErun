Chronic kidney disease is staged by eGFR and urine albumin, and both are monitored regularly because together
they predict progression and cardiovascular risk.

An ACE inhibitor or ARB is standard in chronic kidney disease with albuminuria, titrated to the highest
tolerated dose, with potassium and creatinine checks.

SGLT2 inhibitors are now part of standard chronic kidney disease care for many patients, with or without
diabetes, because they slow kidney function decline.

In chronic kidney disease with type 2 diabetes and persistent albuminuria, a non-steroidal mineralocorticoid
receptor antagonist may be added for extra kidney and heart protection.

Chronic kidney disease care also covers blood pressure, statins, anemia and bone-mineral management, medication
dose adjustment, and, as kidney failure nears, planning for dialysis, transplant, or conservative care.
