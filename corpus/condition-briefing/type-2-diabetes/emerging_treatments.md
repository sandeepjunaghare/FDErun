CWB-214, from Calderwood Bio (fictional), is an oral once-weekly dual incretin agonist for type 2 diabetes in
Phase 3. Its pitch is injectable-level HbA1c and weight reduction without an injection.

MIS-07, from Meridian Islet Sciences (fictional), is a stem-cell-derived islet replacement for insulin-requiring
type 2 diabetes in Phase 1/2. The open question is durability of function without long-term immunosuppression.

OST-330, from Ostrander Pharma (fictional), is a liver-selective glucokinase activator for type 2 diabetes in
Phase 2, aimed at lowering post-meal glucose with a low hypoglycemia rate.

Across the type 2 diabetes pipeline, the trend is toward fewer doses, larger weight effects, and
combinations that pair glucose control with cardiovascular and kidney protection.
